# Team Smandych — Brand kit (provisional)

Rebuilt by Femik from the low-resolution logo files on teamsmandych.ca and Instagram (15 Nov 2026).

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `svg/` | 24 vector files: lockups, wordmark, emblem (colour, mono, silhouettes), badges, app icon |
| `png/` | Every SVG exported at 512, 1024 and 2048 px wide, plus `emblem-original-small-raster.png` (the original drawing, cleaned, for sizes under ~150 px) |
| `favicon/` | favicon.ico + 16–512 px icons |
| `photos/` | 3 full-resolution gym photos + the owners' photo (cleaned up from a screen capture) |

**How it was made**
- Wordmark (“TEAM SMANDYCH / muay thai & kickboxing”): 268 video frames stacked to remove compression noise, then traced to vector. Faithful to the original lettering.
- Elephant: same stacked source, colour-traced into a 9-colour vector. It's a reconstruction of a ~160 px image, not the original illustration.
- Badge: elephant + clean lettering (Inter Display Black, converted to outlines).

**Colours:** black `#0b0b0b` · brand green `#34b25a` · deep green `#0f3d22` · glove red `#c8202f` · silver `#c9ccd1` · white `#ffffff` (gold `#d4a72c` kept as an optional accent)

**Colour roles on the site:** red = action (book, call) · green = brand and information (labels, status, accents) · silver = secondary text.
