Team Smandych — Brand kit

Vector (SVG) and PNG versions of the Team Smandych logo, rebuilt by Femik from the low-resolution files on the current website and Instagram. PNGs come in 512, 1024 and 2048 px wide, transparent unless the name says "on-black"/"on-white".

Provisional: the lettering is a vector trace of the original; the elephant is a vector reconstruction from a ~160 px source. When the gym finds the original artwork files, replace these.
